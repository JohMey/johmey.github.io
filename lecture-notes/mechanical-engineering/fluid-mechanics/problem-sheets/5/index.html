Move the point of application

A rectangular 0.40 m chord, 1.0 m span panel has pressure-coefficient difference falling linearly from 1.4 at the leading edge to zero at the trailing edge. The dynamic pressure is 300 Pa. Estimate lift, centre of pressure and moment magnitude about the quarter-chord position.

Reveal the worked solution

The average coefficient difference is 0.70, so \(L=300(0.4)(0.70)=84\,\mathrm N\). The triangular load acts one third of the chord from the larger end, at 0.1333 m. The quarter-chord is 0.100 m from the leading edge, giving moment magnitude \(84(0.0333)=2.80\,\mathrm{N\,m}\). This is a leading-order thin-panel calculation.

Integrate a two-zone survey

Behind a long cylinder in air at density 1.25 kg/m³, a far-wake survey finds velocity 7 m/s in a central strip 20 mm wide and 9 m/s in two neighbouring strips each 15 mm wide. The undisturbed speed is 10 m/s. Estimate drag per span and the drag coefficient based on diameter 25 mm.

Reveal the worked solution

The pressure-recovered momentum deficit is \(D'=1.25[7(10-7)0.020+9(10-9)0.030]=0.8625\,\mathrm{N/m}\). The reference force per width is \(0.5(1.25)(10)^2(0.025)=1.5625\,\mathrm{N/m}\), giving \(C_D=0.552\). Counting only the central strip misses a substantial share of the deficit.

Open unequal branches

A pump delivers head \(24-0.25Q^2\) metres with Q in litres per second. Static lift is 4 m. Parallel delivery branches have losses \(1.0Q_1^2\) and \(4.0Q_2^2\) metres. Find equivalent resistance, total delivery and flow split. Verify equal branch losses.

Reveal the worked solution

The equivalent coefficient is \((1+1/2)^{-2}=4/9\). The head balance gives \(Q^2=20/(0.25+4/9)=28.8\), hence \(Q=5.367\,\mathrm{L/s}\). Equal branch losses imply \(Q_1=2Q_2\), giving 3.578 and 1.789 L/s. Either branch loses 12.8 m, so the pump supplies 16.8 m including static lift.
