Force between two coaxial coils: a verification series

Two coaxial coils of 100 turns each attract each other; the force is computed for three distances and compared with Maxwell's mutual inductance.

What it computes

OneSimulation solves the 3D vector potential of the two windings with second-order Nedelec edge elements and a Coulomb gauge on one quarter of the air read from STEP; each winding is declared whole and clipped to the quarter by the mesher. The linear systems are solved with GMRES and the auxiliary-space preconditioner for edge elements, and the force is the Lorentz force integrated over each winding. The script that builds the STEP files comes with the notebook.

What it checks

The force on the upper coil matches the exact force from Maxwell's mutual inductance integrated over both winding cross-sections within 0.01 % at 25 mm, 0.09 % (0.03 % on the finer mesh) at 50 mm and 0.34 % at 100 mm, and the forces on the two coils cancel to 2e-4 to 4.5e-3 of the force (Newton's third law).

The geometry script

The script coil_force_verification_geometry.py builds the geometry with the CAD objects of OneSimulation and writes the files. Change a dimension there, run it, and run the notebook again.

"""Geometry of the OneSimulation example "Force between two coaxial coils: a verification series".

Builds one quarter of the air around two coaxial coils with the CAD objects of OneSimulation: a
quarter of a ball of 0.4 m radius, cut by the planes x = 0 and y = 0 (face group planes; the ball
surface is outer), and writes coil_force_verification.step. The quarters of the windings (mean
radius 50 mm, 10 x 10 mm cross-section) are written to coil_force_verification_lower.step (centred
at z = 0) and coil_force_verification_upper_25mm.step, ..._50mm.step, ..._100mm.step for the three
coil distances.
"""

from onesim.cad.d3 import AnnularCylinder, Box, Sphere

air = 0.4
quarter = Box(length=air, width=air, height=2 * air, origin=(0.0, 0.0, -air)).named(
    "cut"
)
region = Sphere(radius=air).named("ball") & quarter
faces = region.boundaries
region.grouped(
    planes=faces["cut.x_min"] | faces["cut.y_min"],
    outer=region.select(faces=lambda face: face.id.startswith("ball")),
).save("coil_force_verification.step")
winding = (
    lambda z: AnnularCylinder(
        inner_radius=0.045,
        outer_radius=0.055,
        length=0.01,
        origin=(0.0, 0.0, z - 0.005),
    )
    & quarter
)
winding(0.0).save("coil_force_verification_lower.step")
for distance in (25, 50, 100):
    winding(distance * 1e-3).save(f"coil_force_verification_upper_{distance}mm.step")

The notebook

The complete notebook, cell by cell. Run it to produce the figures and the comparison printout.

Two coaxial coils of mean radius \(R=50\) mm with a square winding cross-section of 10 x 10 mm and 100 turns each carry 1 A in the same direction and attract each other. The force on a winding is the Lorentz force

\[\mathbf F=\int_V\mathbf J\times\mathbf B\,dV,\qquad F_z=-J\int_V B_r\,dV\]

for the uniform azimuthal current density \(J=NI/A\). The field solves \(\nabla\times(\mu_0^{-1}\nabla\times\mathbf A)=\mathbf J\) with second-order Nédélec elements and a Coulomb gauge; one quarter is computed, and the windings are cut by its two symmetry planes. The coil distance is varied from \(R/2\) to \(2R\), and the middle distance is also solved on a finer mesh.

The air, a quarter of a ball of 0.4 m radius, comes from the CAD file coil_force_verification.step (face groups planes and outer), the quarter of the lower winding from coil_force_verification_lower.step and the upper winding for the three distances from ..._upper_25mm.step, ..._upper_50mm.step and ..._upper_100mm.step. The script coil_force_verification_geometry.py builds them with the CAD objects of OneSimulation.

| face | condition | |---|---| | planes (through the axis), outer | \(\mathbf n\times\mathbf A=0\) |

Reference. Two coaxial loops of radii \(a\), \(b\) at the axial distance \(z\) attract with \(F_z=I_1I_2\,\partial M/\partial z\), with Maxwell's mutual inductance \(M=\mu_0\sqrt{ab}\,[(2/k-k)K(k)-(2/k)E(k)]\), \(k^2=4ab/((a+b)^2+z^2)\) (Smythe, Static and Dynamic Electricity, 3rd ed., Sec. 7.13). Integrating it over both rectangular cross-sections with Gauss-Legendre points gives the exact force between the windings.

import numpy as np
from onesim.cad.d2 import Plane
from onesim.cad.d3 import AnnularCylinder
from onesim.cad.exchange import load
from onesim.materials import VacuumPermeability
from onesim.meshing import LocalRefinement, TetrahedralMesher
from onesim.numerics import Numerics
from onesim.numerics.fem import FiniteElement, Nedelec
from onesim.numerics.linear import GMRES, AuxiliarySpaceMaxwell
from onesim.physics.magnetostatics import (
    CoulombGauge,
    MagneticInsulation,
    MagneticVectorPotential,
    Magnetostatics,
    SolenoidCurrent,
)
from onesim.simulation import Problem, Simulation
from onesim.visualization import plots, theme
from scipy.special import ellipe, ellipk

theme.set()

Geometry and mesh

Each winding is declared whole, as SolenoidCurrent with its own annular support; with clip_regions=True the mesher keeps the quarter of it that lies inside the air region as an embedded region. The cells are 4 mm (2.8 mm on the finer mesh) in and around the windings and grow towards the outer boundary.

air = load("coil_force_verification.step")
faces = air.boundaries
radius, width, turns, current = 0.05, 0.01, 100, 1.0
cases = [(0.025, 0.004), (0.05, 0.004), (0.05, 0.0028), (0.10, 0.004)]
lower = load("coil_force_verification_lower.step")
windings = [
    (lower, load(f"coil_force_verification_upper_{round(1e3 * d)}mm.step"))
    for d, _ in cases
]
coils = [
    tuple(
        SolenoidCurrent(
            inner_radius=radius - width / 2,
            outer_radius=radius + width / 2,
            length=width,
            turns=turns,
            current=current,
            center=(0.0, 0.0, z),
            support_factory=AnnularCylinder,
        )
        for z in (0.0, d)
    )
    for d, _ in cases
]
meshes = [
    TetrahedralMesher(
        target_size=0.06,
        embedded_regions=tuple(coil.support for coil in pair),
        clip_regions=True,
        refinements=tuple(
            LocalRefinement(region=coil.support, target_size=h) for coil in pair
        ),
        size_field=lambda points, d=d, h=h: np.clip(
            h
            + 0.6
            * np.maximum(
                np.hypot(
                    np.hypot(points[:, 0], points[:, 1]) - radius, points[:, 2] - d / 2
                )
                - d / 2
                - width,
                0,
            ),
            h,
            0.06,
        ),
    ).generate(air)
    for (d, h), pair in zip(cases, coils)
]

view = plots.Camera(elevation=20, azimuth=-60)
fig = plots.figure(1, 2, size=(14, 6))
plots.geometry(
    [coil.named(name) for coil, name in zip(windings[1], ("lower coil", "upper coil"))],
    camera=view,
    title="The two windings, 50 mm apart",
    ax=fig[0],
)
plots.mesh(
    meshes[1],
    groups=("all",),
    legend=False,
    camera=view,
    title=f"{meshes[1].cell_count} tetrahedra",
    ax=fig[1],
)
plots.show()

Model

Each winding carries \(NI=100\) ampere-turns spread uniformly over its cross-section (SolenoidCurrent, with the same dimensions as the STEP windings). The linear systems are solved with GMRES and the auxiliary-space preconditioner for edge elements (AuxiliarySpaceMaxwell: a discrete gradient and a nodal interpolation with algebraic multigrid on the nodal spaces).

numerics = Numerics(
    spatial=FiniteElement(element=Nedelec(order=2)),
    linear_solver=GMRES(preconditioner=AuxiliarySpaceMaxwell()),
)
models = [
    Magnetostatics(
        domain=air.volume,
        permeability=VacuumPermeability(),
        formulation=MagneticVectorPotential(gauge=CoulombGauge()),
        current_sources=pair,
    )
    for pair in coils
]
fields = [
    Simulation(
        problem=Problem(
            name="Two coils",
            geometry=air,
            physics=(model,),
            boundary_conditions=(
                MagneticInsulation(boundary=faces.planes | faces.outer, physics=model),
            ),
        ),
        mesh=mesh,
        numerics=numerics,
    )
    .solve_stationary()
    .field(model.magnetic_flux_density)
    for model, mesh in zip(models, meshes)
]

Results

The force on each winding, \(F_z=-J\int B_r\,dV\), integrated over the cells of the winding (its embedded region, region=coil.support), four times the quarter, against the exact force.

mu0 = float(VacuumPermeability().evaluate())
density = turns * current / width**2
radial = lambda B, p: (B[:, 0] * p[:, 0] + B[:, 1] * p[:, 1]) / np.hypot(
    p[:, 0], p[:, 1]
)
forces = [
    [
        -4 * density * field.integrate(region=coil.support, component=radial).value
        for coil in pair
    ]
    for field, pair in zip(fields, coils)
]
nodes, weights = np.polynomial.legendre.leggauss(8)
r, z = np.meshgrid(radius + width / 2 * nodes, width / 2 * nodes, indexing="ij")
w = (np.outer(weights, weights) * (width / 2) ** 2 * density).ravel()
mutual = lambda a, b, s: (
    lambda k: mu0
    * np.sqrt(a * b)
    * ((2 / k - k) * ellipk(k * k) - 2 / k * ellipe(k * k))
)(np.sqrt(4 * a * b / ((a + b) ** 2 + s**2)))
exact = lambda d: np.sum(
    w[:, None]
    * w[None, :]
    * (
        mutual(
            r.ravel()[:, None],
            r.ravel()[None, :],
            d + z.ravel()[None, :] - z.ravel()[:, None] + 1e-9,
        )
        - mutual(
            r.ravel()[:, None],
            r.ravel()[None, :],
            d + z.ravel()[None, :] - z.ravel()[:, None] - 1e-9,
        )
    )
    / 2e-9
)
print(
    "distance   cell size   tetrahedra   force on the upper coil [mN]   exact [mN]   deviation   (F_lower + F_upper) / F_upper"
)
for (d, h), mesh, (f_lower, f_upper) in zip(cases, meshes, forces):
    print(
        f"{1e3 * d:6.0f} mm {1e3 * h:8.1f} mm {mesh.cell_count:12d} {1e3 * f_upper:22.4f} {1e3 * exact(d):17.4f} {100 * (f_upper / exact(d) - 1):+10.3f} %"
        f" {(f_lower + f_upper) / f_upper:+16.1e}"
    )
thin = (
    lambda d: current**2
    * turns**2
    * (mutual(radius, radius, d + 1e-9) - mutual(radius, radius, d - 1e-9))
    / 2e-9
)
print(
    "thin-loop formula for comparison: "
    + ", ".join(
        f"{1e3 * thin(d):.4f} mN at {1e3 * d:.0f} mm" for d in (0.025, 0.05, 0.10)
    )
)
