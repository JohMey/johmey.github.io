3D case studies

Thermal stress in a cooled turbine stator blade

A nickel-alloy vane cooled through three internal channels. The study checks heat balance and compares peak temperature and channel-wall stress on meshes of up to 55,108 tetrahedra.

Thermal warpage of a printed circuit board

A copper-clad board heated to 260 °C. The computed corner deflection is −304.3 µm against −304.8 µm from classical laminate theory.

Heat sink in a forced air flow

A 1 W component cooled by an aluminium heat sink in a laminar air duct. The outlet temperature rise is 14.19 K compared with 13.76 K from the energy balance.

Force between two coaxial coils: a verification series

Two 100-turn coils attract across an air gap. At a separation of 50 mm, the computed force agrees with the mutual-inductance reference to 0.03% on the finer mesh.

Transmission loss of an expansion-chamber muffler

Offset inlet and outlet pipes excite a cross mode above 1,256 Hz. The 3D transmission loss then departs from the plane-wave formula by up to 14 dB.

Oblique whistler and ion-cyclotron waves in Hall MHD

Fourteen wave modes in a 3D periodic plasma test the Hall-MHD dispersion relation, polarisation and magnetic-field divergence.

Recorded session

One request. The assistant builds, runs and reads the study.

Claude Code received this one request and nothing else. Through the OneSimulation MCP server it created the model, started the run and read the temperature field. The centre comes out at 325.0 K, the value of the exact linear profile.

Set it up in your assistant

  1. You

    Create and save a steady heat study for a 2 × 1 × 1 m block with two cells per axis. Use conductivity 4 W/(m·K), density 1,000 kg/m³ and heat capacity 500 J/(kg·K). Hold the left end at 350 K and the right end at 300 K. Insulate the other faces. Run the study and return its run ID, the temperature at the centre and the path to the HDF5 result.

  2. create_simulation

    Box 2 × 1 × 1 m with 2 × 2 × 2 cells. Heat conduction, 4 W/(m·K). 350 K at x_min, 300 K at x_max.

    Project and study saved

  3. start_project_simulation

    Run b318a543-bafd-4df3-b668-6aa26d0cbd4d queued

  4. get_simulation_results

    Completed. Fields: temperature and reaction heat flow on 27 nodes

  5. get_simulation_results · temperature

    Nine nodes each at 350.0 K, 325.0 K and 300.0 K

  6. Claude Code

    The study is saved and the run completed, with the solver converged and the result accepted.

    • Run ID: b318a543-bafd-4df3-b668-6aa26d0cbd4d
    • Centre temperature: 325.0 K (node 13 at the block centre)
    • HDF5 result: …/mcp_runs/b318a543-…/result_sets/5b8d7385-….h5

    The centre value matches the expected linear profile between 350 K and 300 K; every node on the mid-plane reads 325 K.
