Pole geometry and plant uncertainty

Find which poles are achievable, then test what happens when the plant deviates from the nominal design.

Problem sheet 4 · Chapters 7 and 8

1. A position loop with one gain2. An initial movement in the wrong direction3. How much complex model error fits?

1. A position loop with one gain

Take \(P=1/[s(s+6)]\) and \(u=K(r-y)\). (a) Find the breakaway gain and pole location. (b) Choose the gain that gives damped frequency 4 rad/s. (c) Add velocity feedback and select both gains for poles at \(-4\pm3i\).

Compare the desired monic polynomial with the plant

The roots are \(-3\pm\sqrt{9-K}\); they meet at \(-3\) for \(K=9\). A damped frequency of 4 requires \(K=25\), yielding poles \(-3\pm4i\). The second desired pair has polynomial \(s^2+8s+25\). With \(u=K_p(r-y)-K_v\dot y\) its coefficients give

\[K_v=2,\ K_p=25.\]

Both designs have the same natural frequency but different envelopes and peak times.

2. An initial movement in the wrong direction

Let \(G=(1-s/2)/(1+s)^2\). (a) Determine the final response and initial slope for a unit step. (b) Obtain the full step response. (c) Find the time and value of its minimum.

Resolve the step into a repeated-pole response

The DC value is 1 and \(\lim_{s\to\infty}sG=-1/2\), giving the initial slope. Partial fractions of \(G/s\) yield

\[y=1-e^{-t}-1.5t e^{-t}.\]

Its derivative is \((-0.5+1.5t)e^{-t}\); the minimum occurs at \(t=1/3\) s and equals

\[1-1.5e^{-1/3}\simeq-0.0748.\]

It is a zero-induced inverse response in a stable system.

3. How much complex model error fits?

At one frequency \(L_0=-0.6+0.3i\). (a) Compute \(|S_0|,|T_0|\). (b) Test a multiplicative uncertainty radius of 40%. (c) Estimate a worst-case upper bound on uncertain sensitivity there. Explain what remains to establish robust stability.

Measure the distance to the critical point

The distance \(|1+L_0|=0.5\) gives \(|S_0|=2\) and \(|T_0|=\sqrt{0.45}/0.5=1.342\). The uncertainty product is about 0.537, below one here. The triangle bound yields

\[|S|\le2/(1-0.537)=4.32.\]

Check the full frequency band, nominal internal stability and the properties of the uncertainty class before drawing a loop-wide conclusion.
