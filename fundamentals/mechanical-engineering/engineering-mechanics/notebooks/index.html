Change an input, run the Python cells and compare the result with the derivation. You can also download each notebook for Jupyter.

  • How much torque does the sleeve actually carry?

    The core and the sleeve have equal twist per length because they are bonded. Predict which material carries more torque before editing the radii or either shear modulus. Run all cells after changing an input.

    Open editable notebook · Download .ipynb · Chapter 7 · Torsional stiffness of shafts and closed sections

  • How a beam spreads a point load through an elastic support

    A long rail rests on a strip of elastic material. An instrument foot applies a vertical load near the middle. Which part of the strip carries that load? If the rail were completely rigid the answer would depend on its full length. A flexible rail selects a local length through the competition between bending and support compression. Derive that length, calculate a displacement that can be measured by a dial gauge, and recover the answer independently by minimising the energy of a discrete beam.

    Open editable notebook · Download .ipynb · Chapter 20

  • Where does a circular opening concentrate stress?

    Make a hole in a flat sheet pulled along one direction. Derive the conditions on the empty edge, compute the circumferential stress and check how quickly the disturbance decays as a strain gauge moves into the sheet.
