A hollow drive shaft

A 1.2 m long hollow shaft has outer and inner diameters 40 and 30 mm and carries 160 N m. Use \(G=78\,\mathrm{GPa}\). Find the peak shear stress and twist. If its length doubles, which result changes?

Reveal the worked solution

The polar moment is

\[\pi(0.040^4-0.030^4)/32=1.718\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m^4}.\]

The peak shear is \(160(0.020)/J=18.6\,\mathrm{MPa}\) and twist is

\[160(1.2)/(78\times10^9J)=0.0143\,\mathrm{rad}=0.821^\circ.\]

Doubling length doubles accumulated twist at unchanged torque, but the stress at each section is unchanged.

A box-section seam

A single-cell tube with median dimensions 100 by 60 mm and thickness 1.5 mm transmits 420 N m. Find its circulating shear flow, wall shear and force per fastener for one longitudinal seam at 25 mm pitch. What changes if thickness doubles without changing the median dimensions?

Reveal the worked solution

Using N and mm gives

\[q=420000/(2\times100\times60)=35\,\mathrm{N/mm}.\]

The wall stress is \(35/1.5=23.3\,\mathrm{MPa}\), and one fastener transmits \(35(25)=875\,\mathrm N\). Doubling thickness halves wall stress but leaves the equilibrium shear flow and nominal fastener force unchanged.

Connect a cap plate

A built-up beam carries 8 kN shear. Its second moment is \(12.0\times10^6\,\mathrm{mm^4}\). The cap plate area is 800 mm² and its centroid is 60 mm above the neutral axis. Two equivalent rows of fasteners share longitudinal shear. Find the force per fastener at 40 mm pitch. Determine the largest pitch for a 1.0 kN per-fastener limit.

Reveal the worked solution

The cap first moment is \(800(60)=48000\,\mathrm{mm^3}\), and \(q=8000(48000)/(12\times10^6)=32\,\mathrm{N/mm}\). Each row takes 16 N/mm under the equal-sharing assumption. At the specified pitch that is 640 N per fastener. The theoretical limiting pitch is \(1000/16=62.5\,\mathrm{mm}\); the practical arrangement must also supply the assumed composite action.
