A finite source cools partway

A liquid with heat capacity 2.0 kJ/(kg K) flows at 0.50 kg/s from 400 to 340 K at ambient pressure. The environment is at 300 K. Find released heat rate, maximum useful work rate from this cooling interval and entropy generated if heat goes directly into the environment.

Reveal the worked solution

Released heat is

\[0.50(2)(400-340)=60\,\mathrm{kW}.\]

The drop in flow exergy is

\[0.50(2)[60-300\ln(400/340)]=11.24\,\mathrm{kW}.\]

With direct exchange, the reservoirs and stream generate \(60/300-0.50(2)\ln(400/340)=0.03748\,\mathrm{kW/K}\). Multiplication by 300 K recovers the unused 11.24 kW of work potential.

Compare two exhaust states

At a fixed exhaust pressure \(h_f=190,\ h_{fg}=2400\) kJ/kg. A turbine has \(h_3=3200\) kJ/kg, ideal outlet enthalpy 2200 kJ/kg and isentropic efficiency 0.80. Find shaft work per kilogram, actual exhaust quality and output power at 1.5 kg/s. Which outlet contains the larger liquid fraction?

Reveal the worked solution

The actual work is \(0.8(3200-2200)=800\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}\), so \(h_4=2400\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}\). Actual quality is \((2400-190)/2400=0.921\), and shaft output 1.20 MW. Ideal quality is \((2200-190)/2400=0.838\); the ideal exit has the larger liquid fraction because it has lost more enthalpy in the expansion.

Include pump efficiency

A cycle takes saturated liquid with \(v=0.0010\,\mathrm{m^3/kg}\) and \(h_1=200\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}\) through a 3.5 MPa pressure rise. Pump efficiency is 0.70; turbine inlet and outlet enthalpies are 3000 and 2250 kJ/kg. Find pump input, net work, heat input and thermal efficiency.

Reveal the worked solution

The ideal pump needs \(0.0010(3500)=3.50\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}\); actual input is

\[3.50/0.70=5.00.\]

Thus \(h_2=205\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}\), net work \(750-5=745\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}\) and boiler heat \(3000-205=2795\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}\). Efficiency is 26.65%. Rejection \(2250-200=2050\,\mathrm{kJ/kg}\) closes the cycle energy balance.
