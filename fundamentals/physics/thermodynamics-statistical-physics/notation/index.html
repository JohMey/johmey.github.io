Chemical potential

\[\mu=(\partial F/\partial N)_{T,V}\]

This change in free energy per added particle controls exchange between subsystems. In a finite small system an actual finite difference may matter.

Derivation · Chemical equilibrium and reaction extent

Connected correlation

\[C(r)=\langle s_i s_{i+r}\rangle-\langle s_i\rangle\langle s_{i+r}\rangle\]

Subtract the independent contribution before identifying a decay length. A periodic sample introduces a second propagation distance around the ring.

Derivation · Extracting a length from thermal spin fluctuations

Heat capacity

\[C_V=\operatorname{var}(E)/(k_BT^2)\]

This fluctuation formula uses a canonical equilibrium distribution at fixed volume and a temperature-independent energy operator. A finite reservoir suppresses subsystem fluctuations.

Derivation · How a finite heat bath changes a fluctuation

Helmholtz free energy

\[F=-k_BT\ln Z\]

At fixed particle number and temperature, differentiating with respect to volume yields minus the pressure. The ensemble specifies which variables to hold fixed.

Derivation · Thermodynamic potentials and phase boundaries

Inverse temperature

\[\beta=1/(k_BT)\]

It is conjugate to energy in a canonical weight and has units of reciprocal energy. Temperatures in Boltzmann factors are absolute.

Derivation · Partition functions and thermal fluctuations

Multiplicity and entropy

\[S=k_B\ln\Omega\]

Count microstates compatible with the macroscopic constraints. Distinguishable particles, indistinguishable occupations and a changed energy window need distinct counts.
