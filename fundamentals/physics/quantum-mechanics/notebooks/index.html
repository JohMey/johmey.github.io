Quantum mechanics · Computational notebooks

Change an input, run the Python cells and compare the result with the derivation. You can also download each notebook for Jupyter.

  • Can changing a Gaussian width repair a missing cusp?

    Two normalisable trial families compete for the ground state of a one-dimensional attractive contact potential. Each can vary its width. Predict which family can satisfy the derivative jump before inspecting its lowest energy.

    Open editable notebook · Download .ipynb · Chapter 7 · Estimating a ground-state energy

  • What a spin echo can recover

    A collection of coherent spins can give nearly zero transverse signal because the phases of its members disagree. Reverse just one part of the evolution with a pulse and ask how much signal returns. The time dependence of the answer turns an apparent loss of coherence into a measurement of environmental memory.

    Open editable notebook · Download .ipynb · Chapter 20

  • Can an atom leave its initial state and still pass the final test?

    A driven two-level atom undergoes repeated ideal measurements. Compare two laboratory records, one retaining only runs that passed every test and another retaining every run. The distinction is visible even when every measurement is made with the same apparatus.

    Open editable notebook · Download .ipynb · Computational quantum experiments

  • Measure geometric phase from three spin overlaps

    A numerical eigensolver is free to choose a new complex phase for every eigenvector. A product of three measured overlaps removes these arbitrary choices. Relate its argument to a spherical triangle, then see why a vanishing overlap destroys phase information.

    Open editable notebook · Download .ipynb · Quantum states, dynamics and measurement
