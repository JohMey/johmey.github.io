Band velocity

\[\mathbf v_n(\mathbf k)=\hbar^{-1}\nabla_{\mathbf k} E_n\]

This is the group velocity of a band wave packet in the ordinary semiclassical approximation. The Fermi surface determines which states can respond at low temperature.

Cyclotron mass

\[m_c=(\hbar^2/2\pi)\,\partial A/\partial E\]

The area is that of a closed constant-energy orbit in reciprocal space normal to the magnetic field. This is a measured orbital mass rather than a universal free-particle parameter.

Derivation · Counting carriers and weighing a cyclotron orbit

Density of states

\[g(E)=dN_{\rm states}/dE\]

State density and state occupation are independent factors. Their product, integrated over a band, gives the mean particle number.

Derivation · Filling electronic states and measuring the Fermi surface

Fermi level

\[f(E)=1/[1+\exp((E-\mu)/k_BT)]\]

At equilibrium a one-electron level at the chemical potential has mean occupancy one half per available state. Spin multiplicity enters the number of states separately.

Derivation · Filling electronic states and measuring the Fermi surface

London length

\[B(x)=B(0)e^{-x/\lambda_L}\]

This is a field profile in a thick homogeneous superconductor in a linear screening regime. A thin slab requires both boundary conditions.

Derivation · Superconductivity, magnetic screening and flux quantisation

Reciprocal lattice

\[\mathbf G\cdot\mathbf R=2\pi n\]

A reciprocal-lattice vector leaves the phase unchanged under every direct-lattice translation. A basis of different atoms can still suppress a reflection.
