Nonlinear equations · Notation and assumptions

Read each defining equation with its assumptions and units, then follow the link to the argument that gives the equation its meaning.

Backward error

\[F(\hat x)+\delta F=0\]

Specify how perturbations in data or equations are measured. A tiny perturbation making the computed value exact can coexist with a large solution error near a fold.

Derivation · Finding a turning point without using the load as a coordinate

Bracket

\[f(a)f(b)<0\]

Continuity is needed to infer a zero between endpoints. A root of even multiplicity may exist without a sign change.

Derivation · Existence, brackets and error certificates

Complementarity

\[x\ge0,\quad y\ge0,\quad xy=0\]

Both quantities are nonnegative and at least one vanishes. Active-set changes can make a smooth Newton model inappropriate across the switch.

Derivation · Inequalities and active constraints

Contraction

\[\|G(x)-G(y)\|\le q\|x-y\|,\quad q<1\]

The map must also keep the chosen complete region invariant to obtain the standard fixed-point guarantee.

Derivation · Fixed-point iteration and contraction

Newton correction

\[J(x_k)s_k=-F(x_k)\]

The correction solves a local linear model. Quadratic local convergence needs a suitable nonsingular derivative and a sufficiently close starting point.

Derivation · Newton’s method for coupled balances

Trust radius

\[\|s\|\le\Delta\]

The local model is minimised on this region. Agreement between predicted and achieved reduction guides the next radius.
