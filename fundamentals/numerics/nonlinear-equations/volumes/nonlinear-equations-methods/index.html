    Contents

    1. Choosing a derivative step in a nonlinear solve

      A smaller finite-difference step eventually makes Newton iterations worse. Derive truncation and evaluation errors, compare a complex perturbation and a directional derivative, and test which numerical derivative is meaningful for the actual residual implementation.

    2. Accelerating fixed points with differences of residuals

      A slowly convergent physical iteration contains information in the direction of successive errors. Use a scalar extrapolation and a small residual-minimisation problem to exploit that information, then test the extrapolated state against the original equation.

    3. Finding another equilibrium after one root is known

      Newton iteration repeatedly finds the same structural equilibrium. Derive how deflation changes its basins while preserving other solutions, use a rank-one Jacobian update, and distinguish a list of discovered equilibria from a completeness certificate.

    4. Count enclosed zeros with a contour before locating them

      A complex residual may have several nearby zeros whose Newton basins are hard to distinguish. Use the argument principle to count them, contour moments to estimate their locations and an independent perturbation test to certify a root cluster.

    5. Where does a complex Newton step take its starting point?

      A single cubic has three equally simple roots. Derive the iteration, colour starting values by destination and use a separate convergence history to distinguish a bad first step from slow local convergence.

    6. Certifying a Newton step before the root is known

      A small correction is meaningful only together with a curvature estimate. Construct a ball that contains a solution and use a frozen linear solve to make damping decisions independent of equation units.

    7. Which correction does an experiment identify?

      Separate parameter combinations visible in a nonlinear fit, pass a regular fold by continuation and decide when to refine a boundary-value mesh before trusting its Newton residual.
