Examples to open first

Bending of a clamped beam

The tip deflection and the deflection line are compared with u_z(L) = -F L^3 / 3EI; within about 0.3 % for the supplied geometry.

Thermal warpage of a printed circuit board

A copper-clad board heated to 260 °C. The computed corner deflection is −304.3 µm against −304.8 µm from classical laminate theory.

Force between two coaxial coils: a verification series

Two 100-turn coils attract across an air gap. At a separation of 50 mm, the computed force agrees with the mutual-inductance reference to 0.03% on the finer mesh.

Transmission loss of an expansion-chamber muffler

Offset inlet and outlet pipes excite a cross mode above 1,256 Hz. The 3D transmission loss then departs from the plane-wave formula by up to 14 dB.
