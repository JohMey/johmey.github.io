SymptomCheck in order
Validation failsDomain and selection ownership, fields initialized for their roles, support for each boundary and spatial method, solver and scalar type.
Singular or inconsistent solvePressure, potential or rigid-body nullspaces, gauge constraints, global source–flux balance and redundant conditions.
Transient diverges or clips the stepPhysical source scales, explicit stability and CFL, stage solver residual, time-step limits and mesh resolution.
Apparently smooth but wrong fieldCarrier type, coordinates, units, component order, real versus complex representation and reference sampling points.
Fine and coarse pictures differMatch physical points and color limits. Compute the observable and error on both meshes using the same definition.
Results missing at a requested timeCheck accepted output times, retention policy and which references were written to the sink.
