How much of the cross-section has yielded?

A rectangular strip 12 mm wide and 30 mm deep has elastic–perfectly plastic yield stress 200 MPa. Determine the first-yield and plastic moments. At a curvature three times the yield curvature, find the depth of the elastic core and resulting moment.

Reveal the worked solution

With \(c=15\,\mathrm{mm}\), \(M_p=b\sigma_Yc^2=540\,\mathrm{N\,m}\) and \(M_Y=2M_p/3=360\,\mathrm{N\,m}\). The elastic half-depth is \(a=c/3=5\,\mathrm{mm}\), giving a 10 mm deep core. The moment is \(12(200)(15^2-5^2/3)=520\,\mathrm{N\,m}\). Yield has spread far inward for a comparatively small increase over the first-yield moment.

Changing the inspection threshold

A wide plate with a central through crack has applicable toughness 45 MPa√m at the service temperature. With remote tension 180 MPa and \(Y=1\), calculate critical half-length and full length. If inspection finds a full crack length of 16 mm, what remote stress gives the critical intensity?

Reveal the worked solution

The critical half-length is \(a_c=(45/180)^2/\pi=0.0199\,\mathrm m\), or 19.9 mm; the full length is 39.8 mm. The measured half-length is 8 mm, so \(\sigma_c=45/\sqrt{\pi(0.008)}=284\,\mathrm{MPa}\). Both predictions assume small-scale yielding and adequate plate width. Using full length inside the square root would underpredict the critical stress.

Infer release rate from two readings

A specimen with one advancing crack front has thickness 10 mm. Its measured compliance increases from 0.0250 to 0.0270 mm/kN as crack length rises by 1.0 mm. Under a fixed load of 4 kN estimate release rate. For plane stress with \(E=70\,\mathrm{GPa}\), find the corresponding intensity.

Reveal the worked solution

The slope is \(0.0020\,\mathrm{kN^{-1}}=2.0\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{N^{-1}}\). Thus \(G=4000^2(2.0\times10^{-6})/[2(0.010)]=1600\,\mathrm{J/m^2}\). Then \(K=\sqrt{EG}=10.58\,\mathrm{MPa\sqrt m}\). This uses a local finite-difference slope, so significant curvature of the compliance data would call for measurements at closer crack lengths.
