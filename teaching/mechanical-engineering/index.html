Mechanical engineering

Which loads can a structure carry, when will a machine vibrate and what limits heat or momentum transfer?

Lecture notesCurriculum references

Lecture notes

  1. Engineering mechanics

    Trace loads through a structure and distinguish equilibrium, stress and deformation.

    ExercisesNumerical experiment

  2. Machine dynamics

    Move from a single oscillator to the natural frequencies and mode shapes of a machine on a flexible frame.

    ExercisesNumerical experiment

  3. Machine elements and contact mechanics

    Determine how load and curvature set the contact area, pressure and stresses between components.

    ExercisesNumerical experiment

  4. Fluid mechanics

    Connect a growing boundary layer to wall shear, momentum loss and drag.

    ExercisesNumerical experiment

  5. Engineering thermodynamics and heat transfer

    Size a heat exchanger from energy balances and determine the temperature limits of the design.

    ExercisesNumerical experiment

  6. Control engineering

    Design feedback for a tracking requirement and examine how loop gain changes speed, offset and stability.

    ExercisesNumerical experiment
