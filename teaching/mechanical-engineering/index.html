Mechanical engineering

Which loads can a structure carry, when will a machine vibrate and what limits heat or momentum transfer?

Lecture notesCurriculum references

Lecture notes

  1. Engineering mechanics

    Draw a free-body diagram and calculate stress and deformation, then determine whether yielding, instability or a growing crack limits a structure.

    Problem sheetsNumerical experiment

  2. Machine dynamics

    Derive equations of motion, identify modes and damping, and use them to design isolation, balance rotors and assess finite-amplitude vibration.

    Problem sheetsNumerical experiment

  3. Machine elements and contact mechanics

    Follow loads through shafts, fasteners, bearings and drives, then use fatigue, lubrication, contact and tolerance models to assess an assembly.

    Problem sheetsNumerical experiment

  4. Fluid mechanics

    Use momentum and energy balances to predict pipe losses, boundary-layer drag, work in a pump and the flow limits of a compressible nozzle.

    Problem sheetsNumerical experiment

  5. Engineering thermodynamics and heat transfer

    Apply energy, entropy and exergy balances to steam cycles, heat exchangers and enclosures, and derive how heat transfer sets operating temperatures.

    Problem sheetsNumerical experiment

  6. Control engineering

    Model a plant, design and test a feedback loop in time and frequency, then design estimators and controllers that handle sampling, actuator limits and interacting channels.

    Problem sheetsNumerical experiment
