A balanced counterflow exchanger

Both stream capacity rates are 1.5 kW/K and conductance \(UA=3.0\) kW/K. Inlets are at 80 and 20 °C. Determine effectiveness, heat rate, both outlets and the local temperature difference. Explain why both end differences agree.

Reveal the worked solution

At \(C_r=1\), \(NTU=2\) and \(\varepsilon=2/3\). The exchanger transfers \((2/3)(1.5)(60)=60\,\mathrm{kW}\). Hot outlet is 40 °C and cold outlet 60 °C. At each end the hot stream exceeds the adjacent cold stream by 20 K. Equal capacity rates produce equal profile slopes when followed along the same physical direction.

Infer a required exchanger size

A hot stream of capacity rate 4 kW/K cools from 100 to 75 °C. A counterflow cold stream enters at 25 °C with capacity rate 2 kW/K. Calculate its outlet, the terminal differences and required \(UA\). What is the effectiveness?

Reveal the worked solution

The duty is \(4(100-75)=100\,\mathrm{kW}\), giving cold outlet \(25+100/2=75^\circ\mathrm C\). Terminal differences are 25 and 50 K. Their logarithmic mean is \((50-25)/\ln2=36.07\,\mathrm K\), so \(UA=2.773\,\mathrm{kW/K}\). The maximum duty is \(2(100-25)=150\,\mathrm{kW}\); effectiveness is 0.667.

A shield in a black enclosure gap

Large black parallel walls face one another at 400 and 300 K. A thin black shield is inserted midway using negligible conducting supports. Find unshielded flux, shielded flux and equilibrium shield temperature. Does equal spacing enter the result?

Reveal the worked solution

The unshielded flux is \(\sigma(400^4-300^4)=992\,\mathrm{W/m^2}\). Each black gap contributes the same unit denominator, halving the flux to 496 W/m². The shield satisfies \(T_s^4=(400^4+300^4)/2\), giving \(T_s=360.3\,\mathrm K\). Equal spacing is unnecessary for two effectively infinite parallel plates whose view factors are unity.
