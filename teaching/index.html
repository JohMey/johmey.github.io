Physics and engineering course notes

Choose a subject to study the governing equations, work through an analytical example and test it with a OneSimulation model.

Mechanics and engineering

  • Solid mechanics

    Stress, strain, equilibrium and beam deflection.

  • Multibody dynamics

    Generalised coordinates, constraints and pendulum motion.

  • Acoustics

    Linear sound, impedance, reflection and cavity modes.

  • Geotechnics

    Effective stress, groundwater flow and consolidation.

  • Systems and networks

    Lumped balances, circuit transients and energy checks.

Fluids and thermal engineering

  • Fluid mechanics

    Mass and momentum conservation, viscous channels and shocks.

  • Heat transfer

    Conduction, convection, radiation and thermal balances.

  • Multiphysics

    One-way and two-way coupling, interface transfer and thermoelasticity.

Electromagnetic, optical and plasma physics

  • Electrostatics

    Poisson’s equation, dielectric interfaces and capacitance.

  • Magnetostatics

    Vector potential, coils, magnetic flux and inductance.

  • Electromagnetics

    Maxwell’s equations, wave propagation, boundaries and skin effect.

  • Optics and photonics

    Refraction, Fresnel coefficients and diffraction.

  • Plasma physics

    Collective oscillations, shielding and particle-in-cell models.

  • Magnetohydrodynamics

    Magnetic induction, Lorentz force and Hartmann layers.

Chemical and biological physics

  • Chemical kinetics and transport

    Reaction balances, equilibrium, timescales and transport.

  • Biophysics

    Transport, cellular and physiological models, and compartment balances.
