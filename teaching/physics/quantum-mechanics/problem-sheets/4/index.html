Energy bounds and semiclassical actions

Construct a bound from a normalized trial state, compare two reflection conditions and infer a potential’s scaling from level spacings.

Problem sheet 4 · Chapters 7 and 8

A polynomial trial state

In an infinite well \(0<x<L\), take \(\psi=Nx(L-x)\). Determine \(N\), compute its energy expectation and compare with the exact lowest level. Use \(\int_0^L x^2(L-x)^2dx=L^5/30\). What does the comparison test?

Show the variational calculation

Normalization gives \(N=\sqrt{30/L^5}\). Since the trial state vanishes at the walls, the kinetic expectation is \(\hbar^2\int|\psi'|^2dx/(2m)\). The derivative is \(N(L-2x)\), whose squared integral is \(N^2L^3/3=10/L^2\). Thus the bound is \(10\hbar^2/(2mL^2)\) and the exact ground energy \(\pi^2\hbar^2/(2mL^2)\). The bound is \(10/\pi^2-1=1.32\%\) high. Correct endpoints and normalization yield a valid test even though the polynomial has a different shape from a sine.

One wall and one smooth turning point

Consider a particle with an infinite wall at \(x=0\) and \(V(x)=Fx\) for positive \(x\). Evaluate the one-way action to the turning point. Using \(\int_0^{x_t}p\,dx=\pi\hbar(n+3/4)\) derive the energy law and the ratio \(E_1/E_0\) in this approximation.

Show the mixed-boundary quantization

The turning point is \(x_t=E/F\). The integral is \(\int_0^{E/F}\sqrt{2m(E-Fx)}\,dx=2\sqrt{2m}E^{3/2}/(3F)\). Hence \(E_n=[3\pi\hbar F(n+3/4)/(2\sqrt{2m})]^{2/3}\), giving \(E_1/E_0=(7/3)^{2/3}=1.76\). The hard wall fixes a node while the smooth turning point contributes one Airy phase. The low-level ratio is approximate because semiclassical assumptions fail near the turning point and there are few oscillations in the allowed region.

Identify the stiffness of a potential

A symmetric smooth well behaves as \(V=g|x|^s\) and high-level energies scale approximately as \(n^{1.50}\). Derive the scaling for general \(s\), identify the exponent and predict how energies at fixed high \(n\) change if \(g\) is multiplied by four.

Show the action rescaling

Set \(x=(E/g)^{1/s}y\). The action becomes a constant times \(\sqrt{m}\,g^{-1/s}E^{1/2+1/s}\). Quantization gives \(E_n\propto g^{2/(s+2)}(n+1/2)^{2s/(s+2)}\) at fixed mass. Solving \(2s/(s+2)=1.50\) gives \(s=6\). A fourfold increase in \(g\) multiplies energies by \(4^{1/4}=\sqrt2\). This inference uses the spatial region sampled by the measured high levels; the central part of an actual well may follow a different law.
