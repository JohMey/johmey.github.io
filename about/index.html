Selected work

OneSimulation

I develop OneSimulation, a Python runtime for setting up and checking physical simulations. Its public collection includes engineering examples, numerical comparisons and teaching notebooks. The examples on this site document the equations, boundary conditions and checks alongside the source code.

Notebook blog · Physics course notes · OneSimulation on GitHub

MATRICS, an implicit hydrodynamics solver

My doctoral work developed a globally implicit Eulerian solver for compressible flows in astrophysics. In the solver, a Newton iteration uses matrix-free Jacobian–vector products. The published verification covers shocks, viscous Taylor–Couette flow, a shear instability and spatial convergence.

Johannes Meyer, Julio David Melon Fuksman and Hubert Klahr, Astronomy & Astrophysics 686, A119 (2024). MATRICS paper

Doctoral dissertation

The dissertation combines solver development, simulations of the Goldreich–Schubert–Fricke instability, and analysis of planetesimal formation in the Solar System. I defended it at Heidelberg University on 10 July 2024. Dissertation in the Heidelberg University repository
